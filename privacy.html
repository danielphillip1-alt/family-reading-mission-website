2. Website access and hosting

Hosting is provided by Render. When you access the site, technical request data such as IP address, timestamp, requested page, browser information and security/error logs may be processed to deliver the pages, keep the system secure and diagnose faults. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in reliable and secure delivery). The hosting provider's actual processing locations, subprocessors and any international transfers are subject to the provider's current contractual arrangements and must be verified. We do not claim exclusively German hosting or processing without evidence.

3. Membership accounts and digital downloads

We process account and checkout email addresses, chosen book language, subscription and payment status, subscription identifiers and delivery records to perform the membership contract, verify paid access, deliver approved books, handle cancellation and support. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Passwords, where accounts exist, are stored as password hashes, not plaintext. Private book files are access controlled. Where required, legally mandated transaction records are retained under Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

4. Payments: Stripe

Stripe processes card and payment information, recurring billing and tax-relevant checkout details. Our service receives subscription/payment status and identifiers, not the customer's full card number. Stripe is an independent provider for certain processing activities; see Stripe's privacy information. Legal basis: contract performance and applicable financial/tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR). International transfers, if any, are governed by the relevant provider arrangements.

5. Transactional book delivery: Resend and email

Transactional messages may be delivered using Resend or configured email infrastructure to send purchased books, acknowledgments and subscription messages. This requires your email address, book language and PDF attachment or download data. Support communications sent to our business Gmail inbox are processed to resolve requests. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for general correspondence. Please do not email passwords or card details.

6. Cancellation and withdrawal requests

When you use our cancellation or withdrawal forms we record the submitted name, email, type of declaration, contract choice, timestamp and an acknowledgment record. These are processed to handle your declaration, meet legal duties and evidence receipt (Art. 6(1)(b), (c) and (f) GDPR). Relevant records may be retained to document contract fulfillment and defend legal claims, subject to applicable retention limits.

7. Cookies, local storage and analytics

Our membership backend can set a secure, HttpOnly session cookie for authentication. The website's language choice may be stored locally in your browser. These functions support requested site operation and do not themselves require marketing consent. We do not currently claim that analytics or marketing trackers have been audited or that a consent banner is unnecessary for every script. Non-essential tracking must not be activated without the required consent. If analytics, advertising pixels or other non-essential technologies are added in future, appropriate disclosure and consent controls must be installed first.

8. Retention

Account, contract, billing, support and technical information is kept only as long as required for the specific purpose or for applicable legal obligations. Session cookies expire after a limited period; contracts and tax documents may be subject to German statutory retention obligations. The site's precise provider log retention and individual deletion schedules still require verification.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent where consent is the legal basis. Contact brathatfashionhouse@gmail.com. You may complain to a competent data protection authority, including the authority in the German state where the responsible operator is established.

10. International audiences and minors

Our books serve families, but subscription purchase and support are intended to be handled by adults. Please do not provide children's personal details in support messages unless strictly necessary. Where processing reaches individuals outside Germany, additional locally applicable data protection requirements may apply.

Compliance follow-up: Complete operator identity, hosting/data-processing agreements, subprocessor transfers, exact retention periods, and implementation testing must be verified. This notice does not certify those items without evidence.
