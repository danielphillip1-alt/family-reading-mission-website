She knows what’s wrong.
Will anyone listen?

When her mother falls gravely ill in Canquelifá, Guinea-Bissau, sixteen-year-old Love recognizes the symptoms from a medical book she’s studied for years. She knows the doctor who wrote it could help. Finding him means leaving home alone with a handful of rice, a hand-drawn map, and a book she can’t afford to let get wet.

Love knows exactly what she wants to say. Getting the words out takes time, and time is the one thing her mother doesn't have.

Day Six

An Unexpected Friend

Love has never had a friend in the traditional sense. Until now, her brother was the closest she’d ever come. She wants to stay long enough to hear that laugh again, but every moment she stays costs her mother time.
