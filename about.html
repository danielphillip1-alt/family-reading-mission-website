Two authors. One mission.

We are Maimuna and Daniel, the two authors behind Authors On A Mission. We write children's books to bring families together through reading and encourage conversations about kindness, inclusion, understanding and belonging.

Why we write

Every child deserves to feel seen. Our stories celebrate differences, inspire empathy and show how small acts of kindness can make a meaningful difference.

A new book each month

Members receive our children's books in English, German or European Portuguese, created for meaningful family reading.

Our giving commitment

When funds are available, we use 10% of net profits to purchase printed books for children in Europe and Africa. This is a commitment based on net profits, not a donation collected at checkout.
