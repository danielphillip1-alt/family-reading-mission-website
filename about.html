A book can change the way a child sees the world — and themselves.

We are Maimuna and Daniel, two authors, two parents, and two people who believe that every child deserves stories that tell them: you matter, your voice matters, and your future is worth dreaming about.

Our mission is especially close to our hearts: to help put as many of our books as we can into the hands of African children, wherever they live in the world. We want children to hold a story of their own, turn its pages, recognize their possibilities, and feel proud of who they are.

Stories with purpose

Our books are created to do more than entertain. They start conversations between children and the people who love them. We explore identity and pride, the importance of family, friendship and belonging, inclusion and empathy, responsible use of artificial intelligence, entrepreneurial thinking, courage, creativity, and the confidence to turn an idea into something real.

We believe that a curious mind needs encouragement, not limits.

In a world filled with distractions, it can be hard to inspire children to pick up a book, ask a question, or create something for themselves. We refuse to give up on that spark. We want reading to feel like discovery, learning like an adventure, and creativity like a power every child already carries. We want families to read together, talk together, and watch their children grow into thoughtful, capable young people.

Our promise to give

When funds are available, we dedicate 10% of our net profits to purchasing printed books for children, with a special focus on African children and communities, in Africa and across the world. This is not an extra charge or checkout donation. It is a commitment to use the success of our writing to open doors for more young readers. The amount and timing of book donations depend on actual profits and available funds.

Every membership helps us keep writing. Every story is a chance to awaken a young imagination. And every book we place into a child's hands is another opportunity to say: keep reading, keep creating, keep trying. Your story is only beginning.
