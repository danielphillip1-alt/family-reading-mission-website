Membership Terms & Conditions

Effective 13 November 2026. These terms describe our digital membership. Your statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.

1. Who we are

Authors On A Mission is a children's-book membership initiative created by Maimuna Phillip and Daniel Phillip. For customer support and contractual messages, email brathatfashionhouse@gmail.com. The legally required operator identity and service address must also be provided separately in a website legal notice (Impressum).

2. Membership and delivery

A paid membership provides access to approved children's books published during the membership reading cycle in the chosen available language: English, German or European Portuguese. We aim to publish one new children's story each month. Educational materials, including a one-week curriculum per book, will be supplied as they are completed and released; please do not treat unreleased materials as already delivered. Books are provided digitally via the membership download service and, where configured, by email. An active paid membership may be required to access or re-download them.

3. Prices, payments and renewals

The monthly membership costs €3.99 and renews monthly unless canceled. The annual membership currently costs €39.99 and is configured as an automatically recurring yearly Stripe subscription. Its renewal terms are under legal and technical review; mandatory German rights apply. The annual option saves about 16.5% compared with twelve monthly payments at €3.99. Prices shown at checkout govern the actual transaction. Stripe securely processes payments; applicable taxes and the total payable amount are displayed at checkout.

4. Cancellation

You may cancel automatic renewal using the membership management link made available after checkout or by contacting us at brathatfashionhouse@gmail.com. Under the current billing setup, cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the already-paid billing period; you keep access until that date. Cancellation and statutory withdrawal are different rights. Contact us if you cannot access the cancellation flow.

5. Statutory withdrawal rights

Consumers generally have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal for qualifying distance contracts. Specific rules apply when digital content is supplied immediately: the right does not automatically disappear just because a PDF is downloaded. Any early expiration of a withdrawal right requires the legally prescribed express consent, acknowledgement and confirmation. This site does not claim that those conditions have already been met. To exercise your rights, send an unambiguous withdrawal request, including your subscription email and purchase date, to brathatfashionhouse@gmail.com. Applicable mandatory law prevails.

6. Personal, non-commercial use

Books and learning materials are for the personal and family use of paying members. Please do not sell, publicly redistribute, upload to public file-sharing services, or commercially reproduce our books without permission. Statutory exceptions, including applicable educational and copyright exceptions, remain unaffected.

7. Book delivery, problems and statutory remedies

If a download link fails or the wrong language is delivered, contact us with your membership email, book title and selected language. We will verify membership before resending protected files. Your statutory rights concerning defective or unavailable digital content remain in place.

8. Charitable mission

When funds are available, our intention is to use 10% of net profits to buy printed children's books, with particular focus on African children and communities worldwide. This is funded from our profits, not an added checkout donation. The amount and timing of distributions depend on actual funds; no particular donation or distribution is guaranteed.

9. Privacy and contact

Membership and payment data are processed to provide the service and manage subscriptions. Payment details are handled by Stripe. Further information must be supplied in a separate privacy notice. For questions, cancellations or withdrawal notices, email brathatfashionhouse@gmail.com.
