Widerrufsbelehrung und Widerrufsfunktion

For consumer contracts concluded online, you generally have the right to withdraw within 14 days without giving a reason, subject to the mandatory statutory rules for paid digital content and recurring services. The period generally starts upon conclusion of the contract, not before all mandatory withdrawal information has been provided.

Folgen des Widerrufs

Bei einem wirksamen Widerruf werden empfangene Zahlungen grundsätzlich nach den gesetzlichen Vorgaben zurückerstattet, üblicherweise unverzüglich und spätestens binnen 14 Tagen nach Eingang der Widerrufserklärung, vorbehaltlich gesetzlicher Ausnahmen. Any legally permissible amount owed for services already provided depends on statutory requirements; a PDF download alone is not taken as proof that the withdrawal right has expired. Any legally effective early expiry of a statutory withdrawal right for digital content requires the applicable express consent, acknowledgement and contractual confirmation.
